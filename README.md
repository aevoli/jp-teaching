# にほんご 🇯🇵 — Japanese Teaching App

An interactive web app for teaching introductory Japanese to UK primary school children (ages 5–10), designed to run on an interactive whiteboard during 30–45 minute lessons.

---

## How It Works

The app is organised into **3 age bands**. At the start of each lesson, the teacher opens the relevant band and presents the 3 activity options on the whiteboard. Children vote by raising their hands, and the winning activity is launched.

Each activity is fully interactive — children can tap the whiteboard directly to hear Japanese words spoken aloud, play games, and explore the language.

---

## Activities

### 🌸 Band A — Reception / Year 1 (Ages 5–6)

- **Animal Sounds (どうぶつのこえ)** — Tap an animal to hear its Japanese name, then reveal the funny sound it makes in Japanese (e.g. dogs say ワンワン _wan-wan_ instead of woof!). Pairs with a call-and-response game where children act out the animals.

- **Colour Hunt (いろさがし)** — A spinning wheel lands on a colour and announces it in Japanese. Children then race to find something that colour in the classroom. All colours are shown as a tap-to-hear grid.

- **Greetings Song (あいさつのうた)** — Flashcard-style carousel of Japanese greetings (hello, goodbye, thank you, etc.) with the correct action to perform (bowing, waving). Includes an "all together" mode showing every greeting at once for group practice.

---

### ⭐ Band B — Year 2 / Year 3 (Ages 6–8)

- **Number Ninja (かずゲーム)** — Two modes: a learn mode showing numbers 1–10 in Japanese with kanji, and a team game where the app calls out a number in Japanese and two teams race to identify it. Tracks team scores.

- **Body Parts Dance (からだのうた)** — A Japanese "Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes" activity. The app cycles through body part words with animated highlighting at four adjustable speeds (slow → ninja). Children touch the correct body part as each word plays.

- **Food Market (おみせやさん)** — A Japanese food stall role-play. Children tap food items to hear their names and practise ordering ("〇〇をください" — "\_\_ please!"). Includes a role-play phrases panel for shopkeeper and customer exchanges.

---

### 🎌 Band C — Year 4 / Year 5 / Year 6 (Ages 8–10)

- **Hiragana Detective (ひらがなたんてい)** — Children learn 15 basic hiragana characters by tapping to hear them, then use a reference grid to decode secret messages written in hiragana (e.g. spelling out _sushi_, _neko_, _sakura_). Includes hints and multiple messages of increasing difficulty.

- **Self-Introduction Builder (じこしょうかい)** — Children type their name, pick their age and choose something they like from a picture grid. The app generates their full Japanese self-introduction in hiragana and romaji, reads it aloud, and gives presentation tips for delivering it to the class.

- **Kanji Origins (かんじのひみつ)** — Explores how 7 common kanji (mountain, river, tree, sun, moon, fire, water) evolved from ancient pictographs. Children tap to reveal the modern kanji from its picture origin, then test themselves in a multiple-choice quiz.

---

## Key Features

- **Tap to hear** — every Japanese word can be spoken aloud by tapping it
- **Whiteboard-friendly** — large buttons, high contrast, no fiddly hover menus
- **Works offline** — no internet required on the day (audio uses the device's built-in Japanese voice)
- **Teacher controls** — speed settings, score tracking, reset buttons throughout
- **Physical backup** — every activity is designed to work alongside printed flashcards if technology fails
